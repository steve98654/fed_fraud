# SBA-1: SBA screens extended to 7(a), 504 and 8(a)

**For:** SBA OIG, SBA Office of Credit Risk Management. **Weeks:** 3-6
(results by 2026-12-11). **Brief:** [SBA](../agencies/sba.md).

## The problem

The SBA work with Rosemary built screens for pandemic loan fraud. Pandemic
programs are closed, but the people, addresses, lenders and agents behind
that fraud did not go away. The same signals can be run on the programs SBA
runs every year, where catching fraud early prevents losses instead of
chasing them.

## Data

- 7(a) and 504 loan-level data FY2010-FY2026 (FOIA releases).
- PPP and EIDL data already held by the SBA team.
- 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB and VetCert certification lists; SBA OIG reports.

## Detection method

1. **Same screens, new programs:** the existing SBA identifier and address
   screens applied to 7(a) and 504.
2. **Lender and agent concentration:** each lender's share of loans with
   pandemic fraud signals, and charge-off rates against peers.
3. **Cross-program reuse:** borrowers and owners in pandemic fraud findings
   who now hold 7(a) or 504 loans or contracting certifications (SBA-3).

## Validation

Back-test against existing SBA results and SBA OIG cases. PPP and EIDL
fraud carries a ten-year limitation period (2022 law), so pandemic leads
stay prosecutable to 2030-2032.

## Exposure (public figures, not savings)

- PPP loan forgiveness: 19.2% improper; PPP guaranty purchases: 13.3%;
  Shuttered Venue Operators Grant: $10.1B improper (68.9%) (GAO, FY2025).
- Savings from this work: **Pending.**

## What we ask for

- **Now:** confirmation of the SBA OIG contact for referrals; current 7(a)
  and 504 releases if newer than the public files.
- **Phase 2:** loan applications and lender files, to score applications
  rather than loans.

## What you get, and when

By week 6: SBA results across all loan programs, a lender risk table
(shared with FDIC) and version 1 of the SBA playbook that the other 32
agencies reuse.

## Limits

Loan-level public data omit personal identifiers, which limits identity
matching until Phase 2.
