# DOT-1: DBE front and bid rotation screen

**For:** DOT OIG, FHWA Office of Civil Rights, state DOT DBE offices, DOJ
Procurement Collusion Strike Force. **Weeks:** 7-10 (results by
2027-01-22). **Brief:** [DOT](../agencies/dot.md).

## The problem

Federal-aid highway contracts must give a share of work to Disadvantaged
Business Enterprises. Fronts collect that share while the prime does the
work, and bidders in some markets take turns winning. Both are recurring
DOT OIG and DOJ cases. State bid tabulations and DBE directories, which are
public, show who bids, who wins and who is certified.

## Data

- Unified Certification Program DBE directories for three states (for
  example Pennsylvania, Illinois and California).
- State DOT bid tabulations for the same states, 2019-2026 (all bids per
  letting).
- USAspending FHWA, FTA and FAA grants and subawards; state DBE review
  files (FOIA).

## Detection method

1. **Front signals:** DBEs that share addresses, phones or officers with a
   prime; DBEs that only ever work for one prime.
2. **Bid rotation:** complementary bids, stable bid ranks and bidders that
   never beat each other.
3. **Participation gap:** DBE commitments at award that never show up in
   subaward reports (DOT-3).

## Validation

Back-test on charged DBE cases from the case library and state
decertifications.

## Exposure (public figures, not savings)

- DOT outlays over $125B a year, most of it federal-aid construction.
- DOT reports no high-risk improper payment program; DBE and bid-rigging
  losses sit outside payment sampling.
- Savings from this work: **Pending.**

## What we ask for

- **Now:** a DOT OIG contact and one cooperating state DOT; that state's
  DBE review and decertification files.
- **Phase 2:** prime-to-DBE payment records, to show money in against work
  done.

## What you get, and when

By week 10: a DBE integrity screen and bid rotation report for three
states, and a method note other states can run.

## Limits

Bid tabulation formats differ by state. Repeat teaming between a prime and
a DBE is often legitimate; it is a signal only alongside others.
