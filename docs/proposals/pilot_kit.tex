\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section*{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Bottom line}

A leader can say yes to 90 days, a small team, no new system in their
boundary and a clear stop rule. This kit gives them exactly that: a
week-by-week plan, the data-sharing paperwork as fill-in templates, the
metrics that decide whether to scale, and the staffing on both sides.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Why HHS first:} the largest improper payment base (\$28.8B
  Medicare fee-for-service, \$37.4B Medicaid), the highest modeled
  return (see the \href{roi_model.pdf}{ROI model}), a live public
  problem (the 2026 hospice and home health moratorium) and an OIG that
  already runs data analytics.
\item
  \textbf{Why SBA as the alternate:} the SBA work with Rosemary is the
  proof of method, the OIG knows the pandemic cases, and the data is
  already understood. It is the faster yes, but the smaller prize.
\item
  \textbf{Stop rule:} at day 75, if the screen's top 5\% does not
  contain known cases at five times the base rate, or reviewers confirm
  fewer than one in five leads as worth opening, the pilot ends and the
  report says why.
\end{itemize}
