# FCC (Universal Service Fund): one-page brief

**Priority:** 8 of 33 (score 3.5). **FY2026 outlays:** $10.1B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

Almost all FCC spending is the Universal Service Fund, run by USAC: E-Rate
for schools and libraries, Lifeline for low-income phone service, Rural
Health Care and High Cost support. USAC publishes unusually detailed data,
down to each E-Rate funding request, service provider and school. That makes
the FCC a contained, data-rich pilot where the SBA patterns (shared
addresses, related bidders, ineligible recipients) apply well.

## Exposure

- $10.1B in FY2026 outlays.
- Lifeline: $830M in FY2025 outlays with about 9% improper (about $76M).
- E-Rate kickback and bid rigging cases between providers, consultants and
  school officials are a long-running DOJ case type.

## Public data we can use

- **USAC Open Data:** E-Rate funding requests, commitments and
  disbursements by applicant and provider; Lifeline subscribers and support
  by provider and state; Rural Health Care commitments.
- **FCC National Broadband Map** and Form 477 history.
- **USAC investigation documents** and FCC enforcement actions.

## Detection plays

1. **E-Rate collusion:** the same consultant or provider winning across many
   applicants with one bid, prices far above peers, and providers sharing
   owners with consultants.
2. **Lifeline outliers:** providers with subscriber counts or claims
   per area far beyond eligible population.
3. **Rural Health Care pricing:** urban/rural rate gaps far above peers.

## First prototype

An E-Rate provider and consultant network map for the last five funding
years, ranking relationships by single-bid share and price outliers.

## Limits

Subscriber identities are not public. Bids and contracts are summarized,
not published in full.

## Who to take it to

FCC Enforcement Bureau and OIG, USAC Internal Audit and whistleblower
program.
