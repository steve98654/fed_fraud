\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

Interior's distinctive fraud risk is on the revenue side: companies
underreporting oil, gas and coal royalties owed on federal and tribal
land. Interior publishes company-level revenue data, which makes a
public screen possible. Its spending side (tribal programs, wildfire,
land management contracts) looks like other grant and contract work.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$37B in outlays and \$97B in budgetary resources in FY2026.
\item
  Royalty underpayment and valuation disputes are recurring False Claims
  Act and Office of Natural Resources Revenue enforcement matters.
\item
  Tribal grant and contract programs have recurring OIG findings.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Natural Resources Revenue Data (ONRR):} revenue by company,
  commodity, location and year; production by lease.
\item
  \textbf{USAspending and FPDS} for grants and contracts.
\item
  \textbf{DOI OIG reports} and DOJ settlements.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Royalty gaps:} companies whose reported royalty per unit of
  production falls well below peers in the same basin and period.
\item
  \textbf{Contractor screen:} the shared SAM.gov and FPDS checks applied
  to wildfire and land management contracts.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

A royalty-rate outlier table by company and basin from ONRR public data.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Company-level revenue is aggregated; lease-level sales prices are not
public. Valuation rules are complex, so outliers need expert review.

\hypertarget{who-to-take-it-to}{%
\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}
