(USAspending). \textbf{As of:} 2026-10-27. Public data only.

\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

GSA is the best technical partner in government for this program because
it owns the data spine every brief uses: SAM.gov (entities and
exclusions), FPDS (contracts) and the Multiple Award Schedules that
other agencies buy through. Its own fraud risks are schedule pricing
fraud, purchase card misuse and lease fraud. A GSA pilot improves the
shared data for every other agency at once.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$38B in outlays (leases, Federal Buildings Fund, IT and schedules),
  plus tens of billions a year in orders other agencies place on GSA
  schedules.
\item
  Schedule contractors who misstate commercial pricing or sell
  non-compliant goods are a recurring False Claims Act case type.
\item
  GSA SmartPay purchase and travel cards carry misuse risk across
  agencies.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{SAM.gov:} entity registrations, owners, addresses, exclusions.
\item
  \textbf{FPDS / USAspending:} every contract action, including schedule
  orders.
\item
  \textbf{GSA Advantage and CALC} schedule price data.
\item
  \textbf{GSA lease inventory} (lessor, location, rent).
\item
  \textbf{GSA OIG} reports and DOJ settlements.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Price outliers:} schedule prices far above peers for the same
  item or labor category.
\item
  \textbf{Registration red flags:} many SAM.gov registrations at one
  address, recycled officers across UEIs, and firms that re-register
  after exclusion.
\item
  \textbf{Lease risk:} lessors linked to officials or shell entities,
  and rents far above local comparables.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

A SAM.gov registration integrity report: clusters of entities sharing
addresses, officers or bank-like identifiers, ranked by award dollars.
This is the SBA entity screen run government-wide.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Purchase card transactions and SAM.gov sensitive fields (bank data,
TINs) are not public.

\hypertarget{who-to-take-it-to}{%
\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}

GSA Federal Acquisition Service, the Integrated Award Environment
(SAM.gov) team, and GSA OIG.
