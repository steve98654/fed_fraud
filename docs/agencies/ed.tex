\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

Education's measured error rates are low (about 1\% for Pell and Direct
Loans), but ``ghost student'' identity fraud is real and growing. The
department says ID checks saved about \$1B. Public data are
school-level, so we can flag the colleges most exposed, not the fake
students.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Pell Grant: 1.07\% improper in FY2025 (down from 2.64\%). Direct Loan:
  0.99\%. (PaymentAccuracy.gov)
\item
  The department credits mandatory government ID checks with about \$1B
  in savings; before them, under 1\% of students were verified.
  Minnesota alone reported \$12.5M in student aid fraud. (CBS Austin,
  Apr 2026)
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{FSA Data Center:} Title IV volume by school, program reviews,
  and the Heightened Cash Monitoring list.
\item
  \textbf{College Scorecard} and \textbf{IPEDS} (enrollment, online
  share, completion).
\item
  \textbf{Education OIG reports} and DOJ releases.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Ghost student exposure:} colleges, mostly online community
  college programs, where aid volume jumps while completion and
  retention fall.
\item
  \textbf{School risk:} schools on cash monitoring, with sanctions, or
  with owners tied to closed schools.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

A school-level ghost student risk ranking, validated against colleges
named in news and OIG cases.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Student-level data are protected by privacy law and will not be public.
Real detection needs a pilot with FSA.
