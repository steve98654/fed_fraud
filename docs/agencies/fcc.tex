\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

Almost all FCC spending is the Universal Service Fund, run by USAC:
E-Rate for schools and libraries, Lifeline for low-income phone service,
Rural Health Care and High Cost support. USAC publishes unusually
detailed data, down to each E-Rate funding request, service provider and
school. That makes the FCC a contained, data-rich pilot where the SBA
patterns (shared addresses, related bidders, ineligible recipients)
apply well.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$10.1B in FY2026 outlays.
\item
  Lifeline: \$830M in FY2025 outlays with about 9\% improper (about
  \$76M).
\item
  E-Rate kickback and bid rigging cases between providers, consultants
  and school officials are a long-running DOJ case type.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{E-Rate collusion:} the same consultant or provider winning
  across many applicants with one bid, prices far above peers, and
  providers sharing owners with consultants.
\item
  \textbf{Lifeline outliers:} providers with subscriber counts or claims
  per area far beyond eligible population.
\item
  \textbf{Rural Health Care pricing:} urban/rural rate gaps far above
  peers.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

An E-Rate provider and consultant network map for the last five funding
years, ranking relationships by single-bid share and price outliers.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Subscriber identities are not public. Bids and contracts are summarized,
not published in full.
