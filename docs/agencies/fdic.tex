\textbf{Priority:} 27 of 33 (score 2.3). \textbf{FY2026 outlays:} \$4.6B
(USAspending). \textbf{As of:} 2026-10-27. Public data only.

\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

FDIC's spending is mostly the Deposit Insurance Fund resolving failed
banks. Its fraud exposure is insider and borrower fraud that causes or
follows bank failures, and the PPP lending that ran through banks.
FDIC's public bank data is rich; the fit with the SBA work is mainly
through PPP lender behavior.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$4.6B in outlays but \$148B in budgetary resources in FY2026 (the
  fund's balances).
\item
  Bank fraud and PPP-related lender misconduct are FDIC OIG case types.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{FDIC BankFind and Call Reports:} bank financials, failures,
  history.
\item
  \textbf{FDIC enforcement decisions} and OIG reports.
\item
  \textbf{SBA PPP loan data} by lender.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{PPP lender outliers:} banks and fintech partners with
  unusually high shares of PPP loans later flagged in the SBA work.
\item
  \textbf{Pre-failure signals:} banks with rapid growth in risky loan
  types and insider loans before failure.
\end{enumerate}
