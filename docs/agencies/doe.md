# Energy: one-page brief

**Priority:** 11 of 33 (score 3.3). **FY2026 outlays:** $63B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

Most DOE money goes to national lab operating contractors and nuclear
cleanup, which are hard to score from outside. The fraud-prone slice is the
fast-growing grant, loan and small business research money from the 2021
infrastructure law and 2022 Inflation Reduction Act: many new recipients,
large awards, and thin track records. That is where public award data and
the SBA entity checks fit.

## Exposure

- $63B in outlays and $141B in budgetary resources in FY2026, swollen by
  multi-year infrastructure and clean energy appropriations.
- DOE is one of the largest SBIR/STTR funders. GAO found almost 8% of SBIR
  and STTR awardees had four or more fraud risk indicators and about $445M in
  possibly duplicative awards across agencies (2024).
- DOE does not report a high-risk improper payment program.

## Public data we can use

- **USAspending and FPDS:** grants, cooperative agreements, contracts and
  subawards by recipient.
- **SBIR.gov award data:** firm, PI, abstract and agency for every award.
- **DOE Loan Programs Office portfolio** listings.
- **SAM.gov** entity and exclusion records; DOE OIG reports.

## Detection plays

1. **Duplicate research awards:** near-identical SBIR abstracts funded by DOE
   and other agencies (text similarity across SBIR.gov).
2. **Thin recipients, big awards:** new entities winning large grants, shared
   addresses across "independent" applicants, and pass-through subawards.
3. **Excluded links:** recipients or principal investigators tied to excluded
   parties or prior settlements.

## First prototype

A cross-agency SBIR duplicate-award screen covering DOE, NSF, NASA and DoD.
It is cheap (one public data set) and serves four briefs at once.

## Limits

Lab and cleanup contractor costs are not visible below the award level.
Duplicate abstracts are leads; some overlap is legitimate.

## Who to take it to

DOE OIG, DOE Office of Science SBIR/STTR program office, and the SBA SBIR
program office.
