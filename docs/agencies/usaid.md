# USAID (winding down): one-page brief

**Priority:** 24 of 33 (score 2.5). **FY2026 outlays:** $11.7B
(USAspending). **As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

USAID's programs were moved into the State Department in 2025, but the
agency still spent $11.7B in FY2026 closing out awards. Close-out is a risky
period: advances not returned, unsupported final costs, and assets left with
partners. The work belongs with [state.md](state.md) going forward; the
specific USAID task is a close-out recovery screen.

## Exposure

- $11.7B in outlays and $18.5B in budgetary resources in FY2026, mostly
  legacy awards.
- USAID OIG historically reported diversion of aid and partner fraud in
  conflict zones.

## Public data we can use

- **USAspending and ForeignAssistance.gov:** awards, obligations and
  outlays by partner, including terminated awards.
- **SAM.gov** exclusions; USAID OIG reports.

## Detection plays

1. **Unliquidated advances:** terminated awards where obligations far exceed
   outlays, ranked for recovery follow-up.
2. **Partner history:** implementers with prior OIG findings now receiving
   State awards.

## First prototype

A close-out table of terminated USAID awards with remaining balances and
partner risk flags.

## Limits

Close-out financial status is not fully public.

## Who to take it to

State Office of Foreign Assistance, USAID/State OIG.
