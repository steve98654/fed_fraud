\textbf{Priority:} 16 of 33 (score 3.0). \textbf{FY2026 outlays:} \$85B
(USAspending). \textbf{As of:} 2026-10-27 (first written 2026-10-25).
Public data only.

\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

HUD's rental assistance error rate is high (26.4\%), but most of it sits
in tenant income and eligibility data that are not public. What we can
do now is screen the landlords, property owners and disaster grant
recipients that public data do name.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Rental Housing Assistance (Sections 8, 236, 811): \$4.3B improper
  (26.4\%). Tenant-Based Rental Assistance: \$1.5B. (GAO, FY2025)
\item
  Disaster recovery grants (CDBG-DR) and housing grants are recurring
  HUD OIG fraud areas.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Multifamily assisted housing data:} properties, contracts,
  owners and management agents.
\item
  \textbf{Inspection scores} for HUD-assisted properties.
\item
  \textbf{Picture of Subsidized Households} (aggregate tenant data by
  area).
\item
  \textbf{USAspending} grants, and HUD OIG reports.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Owner networks:} owners and managers controlling many
  properties with poor inspection scores while still receiving payments.
\item
  \textbf{Grant sub-recipients:} disaster grant contractors that are
  new, share addresses, or tie to excluded parties.
\item
  \textbf{Area outliers:} subsidy cost per unit far above local peers.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

An owner network view of HUD-assisted multifamily properties joined to
inspection scores and SAM.gov exclusions.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Tenant-level income and payment data are not public, so the main source
of improper payments needs agency data in a pilot.

\hypertarget{who-to-take-it-to}{%
\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}

HUD's Office of Multifamily Housing, the CFO's payment integrity team,
and HUD OIG.
