\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

HHS is where the money and the data meet. Medicare and Medicaid had
about \$90B in FY2025 improper payments, and CMS publishes
provider-level billing, enrollment and ownership data that let us score
individual providers. Start with hospice and home health, which CMS
froze to new Medicare enrollment in May 2026 and DOJ named as
whistleblower priorities.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Medicaid: \$37.4B improper (6.1\%). Medicare fee-for-service: \$28.8B
  (6.6\%). Medicare Advantage: \$23.7B (6.1\%). Head Start: 10.3\% rate.
  (GAO, FY2025)
\item
  CMS imposed a six-month nationwide moratorium on new hospice and home
  health enrollments on 2026-05-13, citing sham hospices and patients
  who were not terminally ill.
\item
  Payment suspensions hit about 800 Los Angeles hospices and home health
  agencies; HHS froze federal child care payments to Minnesota in Dec
  2025.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Medicare Provider Utilization and Payment} (by provider and
  service), Part D prescribers, and the hospice and home health provider
  files.
\item
  \textbf{Provider ownership data} for hospices, home health agencies
  and nursing homes (owners, ownership changes, shared owners).
\item
  \textbf{NPPES} (NPI registry: addresses, taxonomies, enumeration
  dates).
\item
  \textbf{HHS-OIG LEIE} exclusion list and \textbf{Open Payments}.
\item
  \textbf{DOJ and HHS-OIG enforcement releases} as labeled examples.
\item
  Head Start and child care grants on \textbf{USAspending}.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Hospice and home health clusters:} many new providers at
  shared addresses or with shared owners, fast billing ramp after
  enrollment, unusually long stays or live-discharge rates versus peers.
\item
  \textbf{Ownership networks:} owners or managers linked to
  LEIE-excluded parties or to providers already suspended; serial
  ownership changes.
\item
  \textbf{Outlier billing:} providers far above peers in services per
  patient, new high-cost codes, or patterns matching past DOJ cases (for
  example COVID and respiratory test billing).
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

A hospice and home health risk map for two hot spots (Los Angeles County
and Minnesota). Each provider gets a score with the reasons behind it,
and the view shows the owner network. Validate against providers already
suspended or charged.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Public files lag 1 to 2 years and are aggregated (small counts
suppressed). Medicaid claims are state-run and not public, so Medicaid
work starts from provider enrollment and ownership, not claims.
