# Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Trust Fund: one-page brief

**Priority:** 31 of 33 (score 1.8). **FY2026 outlays:** $1.1B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

This trust fund is not an agency in the usual sense. USAspending lists it
separately; it funds the Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute
(PCORI) and related HHS research work. Fraud risk is typical of research
grants: duplicate funding and unsupported costs. It is best folded into the
cross-agency research award screen rather than worked on alone.

## Exposure

- $1.1B in outlays in FY2026.

## Public data we can use

- **PCORI funded projects** (awardee, PI, amount, abstract).
- **NIH RePORTER, NSF Award Search, SBIR.gov** for overlap.

## Detection plays

1. **Duplicate research** across PCORI, NIH and NSF.
2. **Awardee links** to excluded parties (LEIE, SAM.gov).

## First prototype

Add PCORI awards to the cross-agency duplicate-award screen.

## Limits

PCORI is a private nonprofit; its subawards are not in USAspending.

## Who to take it to

PCORI, HHS OIG (for the HHS share).
