(USAspending). \textbf{As of:} 2026-10-27. Public data only.

\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

NASA spends most of its budget on a few very large contracts, where
fraud is about cost mischarging that public data cannot see. The
public-data opening is small business research money (SBIR/STTR) and the
supplier base under the large primes, where the SBA checks apply.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$23B in outlays in FY2026.
\item
  SBIR/STTR duplicate funding and misuse are recurring NASA OIG cases;
  GAO found about \$445M in possibly duplicative SBIR awards across
  agencies.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}

\item
  \textbf{FPDS / USAspending} contracts and subawards.
\item
  \textbf{SAM.gov} entities and exclusions; NASA OIG reports.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Duplicate research awards} across agencies (shared with
  \url{doe.pdf} and \url{nsf.pdf}).
\item
  \textbf{Award-mill firms:} firms with many SBIR Phase I awards and no
  Phase II or commercialization record.
\item
  \textbf{Supplier screen:} subcontractors under large primes tied to
  excluded parties or sharing addresses with the prime.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

Include NASA in the cross-agency SBIR duplicate-award screen.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Cost-plus contract mischarging and labor fraud are invisible in public
data.

\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}
