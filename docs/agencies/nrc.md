# Nuclear Regulatory Commission: one-page brief

**Priority:** 31 of 33 (score 1.8). **FY2026 outlays:** $0.86B
(USAspending). **As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

The NRC is a regulator funded mostly by license fees. Its spending fraud
risk is small and limited to ordinary contracts. Its relevant fraud is
regulatory: falsified safety records and counterfeit or substandard parts in
the nuclear supply chain. That is outside the SBA method, so the NRC is low
priority for this program.

## Exposure

- $0.86B in outlays in FY2026.
- Counterfeit, fraudulent and suspect items in the supply chain are a known
  NRC concern.

## Public data we can use

- **NRC ADAMS** document library (inspection and enforcement records).
- **FPDS** for NRC contracts; **SAM.gov** exclusions.

## Detection plays

1. **Supplier screen:** nuclear-grade suppliers tied to enforcement actions
   or excluded parties.

## First prototype

None proposed; include NRC contracts in the government-wide contractor screen.

## Limits

Supply-chain data is mostly held by licensees.

## Who to take it to

NRC Office of Investigations, NRC OIG.
