\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

State now holds most U.S. foreign assistance after USAID's programs
moved under it in 2025 (see \url{usaid.pdf} for the wind-down). Foreign
aid fraud is diversion, inflated costs and fake beneficiaries through
chains of implementing partners and local subgrantees. Prime awards are
public; the local end of the chain mostly is not. Public data can rank
implementing partners and spot shared ownership, but not verify delivery
on the ground.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$39B in outlays and \$95B in budgetary resources in FY2026, including
  foreign assistance accounts.
\item
  Passport and visa fraud is a separate, revenue and security risk
  handled by Diplomatic Security.
\item
  No State program reports a high-risk improper payment estimate.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{ForeignAssistance.gov} and \textbf{USAspending:} awards by
  implementing partner, country and sector.
\item
  \textbf{FPDS} for embassy construction, security and logistics
  contracts.
\item
  \textbf{SAM.gov} exclusions; State OIG and USAID OIG reports and
  cases.
\item
  \textbf{IATI} data published by implementing partners.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Partner concentration:} implementers that win large shares of
  a country's aid with weak competition, or that share officers with
  subcontractors.
\item
  \textbf{Excluded and sanctioned links:} partners or subgrantees linked
  to excluded parties or to OFAC sanctions lists.
\item
  \textbf{Cost outliers:} overhead and unit costs far above peers for
  the same sector and country.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

An implementing-partner risk profile for the ten largest aid recipient
countries, joining award data, exclusions and sanctions lists.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Subawards to foreign local partners are thinly reported. Delivery cannot
be verified from public data.

\hypertarget{who-to-take-it-to}{%
\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}

State Office of Foreign Assistance, State OIG, and the Bureau of
Diplomatic Security for passport and visa fraud.
