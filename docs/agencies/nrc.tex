\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

The NRC is a regulator funded mostly by license fees. Its spending fraud
risk is small and limited to ordinary contracts. Its relevant fraud is
regulatory: falsified safety records and counterfeit or substandard
parts in the nuclear supply chain. That is outside the SBA method, so
the NRC is low priority for this program.
