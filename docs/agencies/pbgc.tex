\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

PBGC pays pensions for failed plans and, since 2021, has paid Special
Financial Assistance (SFA) grants to rescue multiemployer plans. The SFA
program showed a concrete, public failure: Central States got about
\$127M too much because 3,479 dead participants were counted as alive,
since PBGC was not checking SSA's death file until November 2023. Other
SFA plans may carry the same error.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$10.6B in outlays and \$91B in budgetary resources in FY2026.
\item
  Central States alone received a \$35.8B SFA grant; the \$127M
  overpayment was about 0.35\% of it. PBGC says it will recover funds
  tied to deceased participants from other plans.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{PBGC SFA application and approval listings} (plan, amount,
  date).
\item
  \textbf{Form 5500 filings (DOL EFAST):} participant counts, deaths
  reported, and actuarial data for every plan.
\item
  \textbf{PBGC OIG reports} and congressional oversight letters.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{SFA census checks:} plans whose participant counts or death
  rates in the SFA application differ from their own Form 5500 history
  and actuarial norms.
\item
  \textbf{Benefit payee checks} (with SSA data): payees with no sign of
  life.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

A plan-level table comparing SFA application data with Form 5500 trends,
ranking plans by likely overcounted participants.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Participant-level data is not public; the screen finds plans to audit,
not individual dead payees.

\hypertarget{who-to-take-it-to}{%
\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}

PBGC Multiemployer Program, PBGC OIG, and DOL's Employee Benefits
Security Administration.
