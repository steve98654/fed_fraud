\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

This trust fund is not an agency in the usual sense. USAspending lists
it separately; it funds the Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute
(PCORI) and related HHS research work. Fraud risk is typical of research
grants: duplicate funding and unsupported costs. It is best folded into
the cross-agency research award screen rather than worked on alone.
