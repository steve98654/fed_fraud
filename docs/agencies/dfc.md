# U.S. International Development Finance Corporation: one-page brief

**Priority:** 29 of 33 (score 2.1). **FY2026 outlays:** $1.6B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

DFC lends to, insures and invests in private projects abroad. Outlays are
small, but its budgetary resources ($10.4B) and exposure on loans are much
larger. The fraud risk is borrowers who misstate ownership, use of funds or
project status. Public project data supports a light-touch screen of
borrowers and sponsors.

## Exposure

- $1.6B in outlays and $10.4B in budgetary resources in FY2026.
- Loan and guarantee exposure well above annual outlays.

## Public data we can use

- **DFC active projects list:** borrower, sponsor, country, amount.
- **USAspending** obligations; **SAM.gov** exclusions; OFAC sanctions lists.

## Detection plays

1. **Sponsor screen:** borrowers or sponsors tied to sanctioned or excluded
   parties, or to shell companies in secrecy jurisdictions.
2. **Concentration:** repeat borrowers with shared owners across "separate"
   projects.

## First prototype

A sponsor and borrower sanctions and exclusions check over DFC's project
list.

## Limits

Beneficial ownership of foreign borrowers is rarely public.

## Who to take it to

DFC Office of Accountability, DFC OIG.
