\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

USAID's programs were moved into the State Department in 2025, but the
agency still spent \$11.7B in FY2026 closing out awards. Close-out is a
risky period: advances not returned, unsupported final costs, and assets
left with partners. The work belongs with \url{state.pdf} going forward;
the specific USAID task is a close-out recovery screen.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \$11.7B in outlays and \$18.5B in budgetary resources in FY2026,
  mostly legacy awards.
\item
  USAID OIG historically reported diversion of aid and partner fraud in
  conflict zones.
\end{itemize}

\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}

\item

  receiving State awards.
\end{enumerate}
