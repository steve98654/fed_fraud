# Executive Office of the President: one-page brief

**Priority:** 33 of 33 (score 1.3). **FY2026 outlays:** $0.85B
(USAspending). **As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

EOP spending is small and mostly staff and operations, so it is not a fraud
target. EOP matters as a sponsor: OMB sets improper payment and fraud risk
policy for every agency, and the President launched a nationwide anti-fraud
task force by executive order in March 2026. Treat EOP and OMB as the audience for the
government-wide proposal, not as a subject.

## Exposure

- $0.85B in outlays in FY2026. Includes the Office of National Drug Control
  Policy's grant programs (HIDTA).

## Public data we can use

- **USAspending** for EOP grants and contracts.
- **PaymentAccuracy.gov** (run by OMB) for government-wide improper payment
  data used across this set.
