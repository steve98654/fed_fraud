# State (including absorbed USAID functions): one-page brief

**Priority:** 16 of 33 (score 3.0). **FY2026 outlays:** $39B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

State now holds most U.S. foreign assistance after USAID's programs moved
under it in 2025 (see [usaid.md](usaid.md) for the wind-down). Foreign aid
fraud is diversion, inflated costs and fake beneficiaries through chains of
implementing partners and local subgrantees. Prime awards are public; the
local end of the chain mostly is not. Public data can rank implementing
partners and spot shared ownership, but not verify delivery on the ground.

## Exposure

- $39B in outlays and $95B in budgetary resources in FY2026, including
  foreign assistance accounts.
- Passport and visa fraud is a separate, revenue and security risk handled
  by Diplomatic Security.
- No State program reports a high-risk improper payment estimate.

## Public data we can use

- **ForeignAssistance.gov** and **USAspending:** awards by implementing
  partner, country and sector.
- **FPDS** for embassy construction, security and logistics contracts.
- **SAM.gov** exclusions; State OIG and USAID OIG reports and cases.
- **IATI** data published by implementing partners.

## Detection plays

1. **Partner concentration:** implementers that win large shares of a
   country's aid with weak competition, or that share officers with
   subcontractors.
2. **Excluded and sanctioned links:** partners or subgrantees linked to
   excluded parties or to OFAC sanctions lists.
3. **Cost outliers:** overhead and unit costs far above peers for the same
   sector and country.

## First prototype

An implementing-partner risk profile for the ten largest aid recipient
countries, joining award data, exclusions and sanctions lists.

## Limits

Subawards to foreign local partners are thinly reported. Delivery cannot be
verified from public data.

## Who to take it to

State Office of Foreign Assistance, State OIG, and the Bureau of Diplomatic
Security for passport and visa fraud.
