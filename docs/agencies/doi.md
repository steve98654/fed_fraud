# Interior: one-page brief

**Priority:** 27 of 33 (score 2.3). **FY2026 outlays:** $37B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

Interior's distinctive fraud risk is on the revenue side: companies
underreporting oil, gas and coal royalties owed on federal and tribal land.
Interior publishes company-level revenue data, which makes a public screen
possible. Its spending side (tribal programs, wildfire, land management
contracts) looks like other grant and contract work.

## Exposure

- $37B in outlays and $97B in budgetary resources in FY2026.
- Royalty underpayment and valuation disputes are recurring False Claims Act
  and Office of Natural Resources Revenue enforcement matters.
- Tribal grant and contract programs have recurring OIG findings.

## Public data we can use

- **Natural Resources Revenue Data (ONRR):** revenue by company, commodity,
  location and year; production by lease.
- **USAspending and FPDS** for grants and contracts.
- **DOI OIG reports** and DOJ settlements.

## Detection plays

1. **Royalty gaps:** companies whose reported royalty per unit of production
   falls well below peers in the same basin and period.
2. **Contractor screen:** the shared SAM.gov and FPDS checks applied to
   wildfire and land management contracts.

## First prototype

A royalty-rate outlier table by company and basin from ONRR public data.

## Limits

Company-level revenue is aggregated; lease-level sales prices are not
public. Valuation rules are complex, so outliers need expert review.

## Who to take it to

Office of Natural Resources Revenue, DOI OIG.
