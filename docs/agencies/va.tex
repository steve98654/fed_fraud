\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

VA pairs naturally with DoD. Its service-disabled veteran-owned (SDVOSB)
set-aside contracts use the same SAM.gov and FPDS pipeline, and its GI
Bill school data let us screen schools. Benefit payment fraud itself
(compensation, community care) is mostly hidden from public data.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  VA reported \$3.92B in improper and unknown payments across 7 programs
  in FY2025: \$1.49B monetary loss and \$1.74B unknown. Compensation
  alone was about \$2.3B. (PaymentAccuracy.gov, GAO)
\item
  SDVOSB pass-through and fake-eligibility schemes are recurring VA OIG
  and DOJ cases.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{FPDS / USAspending} VA contracts with set-aside type.
\item
  \textbf{SAM.gov} and \textbf{SBA VetCert} certification lists.
\item
  \textbf{GI Bill Comparison Tool} data (school, enrollment, caution
  flags).
\item
  \textbf{VA OIG reports} and DOJ releases.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{SDVOSB fronts:} certified firms that subcontract most work,
  share officers with non-veteran firms, or appear just before large
  awards.
\item
  \textbf{School risk:} GI Bill schools with fast enrollment growth,
  caution flags, or ownership links to schools that closed or were
  sanctioned.
\item
  \textbf{Community care providers:} cross-match VA community care
  vendors against HHS-OIG's exclusion list (via the HHS pipeline).
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

Add VA to the DoD contractor screen (same code, VA awards) and a GI Bill
school watch list.
