\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

AmeriCorps is small, but one of its programs has one of the highest
reported error rates in government: the Foster Grandparent Program at
23.6\% (FY2025). The usual problems are stipend and timesheet fraud and
grantees that cannot support costs. Public data can rank grantees, not
members.

\item

  FY2025).
\end{itemize}

\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Grantee risk:} grantees with prior OIG findings, shared
  officers across grantees, or rapid growth without matching revenue.
\item
  \textbf{Program comparison:} grantees whose cost per member far
  exceeds peers.
\end{enumerate}
