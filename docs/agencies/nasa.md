# NASA: one-page brief

**Priority:** 16 of 33 (score 3.0). **FY2026 outlays:** $23B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

NASA spends most of its budget on a few very large contracts, where fraud
is about cost mischarging that public data cannot see. The public-data
opening is small business research money (SBIR/STTR) and the supplier base
under the large primes, where the SBA checks apply.

## Exposure

- $23B in outlays in FY2026.
- SBIR/STTR duplicate funding and misuse are recurring NASA OIG cases; GAO
  found about $445M in possibly duplicative SBIR awards across agencies.

## Public data we can use

- **SBIR.gov** award data; **NASA TechPort** project data.
- **FPDS / USAspending** contracts and subawards.
- **SAM.gov** entities and exclusions; NASA OIG reports.

## Detection plays

1. **Duplicate research awards** across agencies (shared with
   [doe.md](doe.md) and [nsf.md](nsf.md)).
2. **Award-mill firms:** firms with many SBIR Phase I awards and no Phase II
   or commercialization record.
3. **Supplier screen:** subcontractors under large primes tied to excluded
   parties or sharing addresses with the prime.

## First prototype

Include NASA in the cross-agency SBIR duplicate-award screen.

## Limits

Cost-plus contract mischarging and labor fraud are invisible in public data.

## Who to take it to

NASA OIG, NASA Office of Small Business Programs.
