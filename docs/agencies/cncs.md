# AmeriCorps (Corporation for National and Community Service): one-page brief

**Priority:** 30 of 33 (score 2.0). **FY2026 outlays:** $1.2B (USAspending).
**As of:** 2026-10-27. Public data only.

## Bottom line

AmeriCorps is small, but one of its programs has one of the highest reported
error rates in government: the Foster Grandparent Program at 23.6% (FY2025).
The usual problems are stipend and timesheet fraud and grantees that cannot
support costs. Public data can rank grantees, not members.

## Exposure

- $1.2B in outlays in FY2026.
- Foster Grandparent Program: 23.6% improper payment rate (GAO, FY2025).

## Public data we can use

- **USAspending grants** to AmeriCorps grantees and subgrantees.
- **AmeriCorps OIG** reports and investigations.
- **SAM.gov** exclusions; IRS Form 990 data for nonprofit grantees.

## Detection plays

1. **Grantee risk:** grantees with prior OIG findings, shared officers across
   grantees, or rapid growth without matching revenue.
2. **Program comparison:** grantees whose cost per member far exceeds peers.

## First prototype

A grantee risk list for the Foster Grandparent and AmeriCorps State and
National programs.

## Limits

Member timesheets and stipend payments are not public.

## Who to take it to

AmeriCorps Office of Grant Administration, AmeriCorps OIG.
