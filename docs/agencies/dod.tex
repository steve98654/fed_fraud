\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

DoD contracting is the fastest technical win. It runs on the same public
data the SBA work already uses (SAM.gov, FPDS, USAspending), and DoD
awards far more contract dollars than any other agency (roughly \$400B+
a year; approximate, from USAspending). Small business set-aside and
pass-through fraud is the direct bridge from SBA's 8(a), HUBZone and
women-owned programs.

\hypertarget{exposure}{%
\section{Exposure}\label{exposure}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  DoD's reported improper payment rates are highest in travel pay (11\%
  to 13\% across systems) and include \$1.4B in one commercial pay
  system (GAO, FY2025).
\item
  The bigger risk is procurement fraud that payment sampling does not
  catch: fake small businesses, pass-through fronts, bid rigging and
  inflated pricing. These are recurring DCIS and DOJ cases.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{public-data-we-can-use}{%
\section{Public data we can use}\label{public-data-we-can-use}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{FPDS / USAspending} contract actions: vendor UEI, set-aside
  type, competition, modifications, subcontract reports.
\item
  \textbf{SAM.gov entity and exclusion records:} addresses, officers,
  business type certifications, registration dates.
\item
  \textbf{SBA certification lists} (8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, VetCert).
\item
  \textbf{DCIS and DOJ press releases} for labeled cases.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{detection-plays}{%
\section{Detection plays}\label{detection-plays}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Front companies:} set-aside winners that are new, share
  addresses or officers with large firms, or subcontract most work to a
  non-eligible firm.
\item
  \textbf{Collusion signals:} ``competitors'' sharing officers,
  addresses or phone numbers, rotating wins, and identical bids.
\item
  \textbf{Award anomalies:} sole-source awards just under thresholds,
  rapid modification growth, and payees linked to excluded parties.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{first-prototype}{%
\section{First prototype}\label{first-prototype}}

A contractor integrity screen over DoD set-aside awards for the last
five fiscal years. Reuse the SBA entity-resolution pipeline, add FPDS
award features, and rank vendors with stated reasons. Validate against
DOJ and DCIS cases.

\hypertarget{limits}{%
\section{Limits}\label{limits}}

Invoices and payments below the award level are not public, so this
finds risky vendors and awards, not individual false invoices.
Classified work is excluded.

\hypertarget{who-to-take-it-to}{%
\section{Who to take it to}\label{who-to-take-it-to}}

The DoD Comptroller and Office of Small Business Programs, DCIS / DoD
OIG, and DOJ's Procurement Collusion Strike Force.
