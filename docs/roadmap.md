# Fraud investigation roadmap: top ideas for the 33 largest agencies

**Status:** Draft v0.1 (2026-11-01), for Steve's review.
**Builds on:** [the top 33 ranking](agencies/top33.md), the 33 agency briefs
in [docs/agencies/](agencies/top33.md) and the
[review plan](review_plan.md), whose weeks, waves and IDs (F1-F12, C1-C3,
P1-P9) this roadmap follows. Public data only in Phase 1; every idea below
produces leads for an Inspector General or DOJ, not public accusations.

## Bottom line

We will run **86 investigation ideas across 33 agencies** in two
phases, but they reduce to a small number of patterns the SBA work already
detects: shell and front companies, shared identifiers across "separate"
recipients, outlier billing or pricing, links to excluded parties, and
payments to people who are dead or ineligible. That is why one shared
pipeline can cover the whole government.

- **Start with ten investigations** (table below). They sit where the most
  fraud-prone money meets record-level public data: HHS hospice and home
  health, DoD set-aside fronts, SBA's non-pandemic loans, customs evasion,
  SNAP retailers and meal sponsors, DOT DBE fronts and SAM.gov shells.
- **Cover the other 26 agencies cheaply** through three shared screens
  (contractors C1, research awards C2, grantees C3), five contained pilots
  and aggregate screens where payee data is confidential.
- **Every agency gets a finding by 2027-02-26** (week 15): a ranked lead list
  or a reasoned "nothing actionable from public data", plus a Phase 2 data
  request.
- **Phase 2 turns leads into cases.** Internal claims, invoices and payment
  files, reached first through OIGs, move the work from risky entities to
  specific false claims. The biggest ideas are cross-agency: death data,
  shared bank accounts and identity reuse.

## How to read the roadmap

Each idea has an ID (agency code and number, such as **HHS-1**) so later
prototypes, memos and proposals can refer to it. "When" gives the Phase 1
weeks from the review plan (week 1 starts 2026-11-02; weeks skip
2026-12-21 to 2027-01-01). "Take to" names who receives the leads. The
first idea listed for each agency is its first prototype from the brief.

Agencies are grouped by the review plan's workstreams: Wave 1 and Wave 2
deep dives (A1, A2), contained pilots (B), shared screens (C1-C3), data
partners (E) and aggregate screens with access cases (D).

## Phase 2: what internal data adds

Phase 1 leads open the door to internal data, starting with OIGs (two to four
months to data, against six to twelve for matching agreements). The six
first access proposals go out in week 8 (2027-01-08, P2); Treasury, SSA and
DOL follow on 2027-02-12 (P4).

| ID | Cross-agency investigation | Internal data | Lead agencies | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X-1 | Payments to dead people in FEHB, PBGC, Medicare, VA and SNAP | SSA full death file | SSA, Treasury Do Not Pay | Late 2027 |
| X-2 | One bank account paid for many unrelated identities or firms | Payment file account numbers | Treasury Fiscal Service | 2028 |
| X-3 | One identity or device behind UI, SNAP, Medicaid, SBA and aid claims | Application identity, device and IP data | DOL, USDA, HHS, SBA, ED via states | Mid 2028 |
| X-4 | Hidden common control across grants, contracts, loans and benefits | Payee TINs, owners, bank accounts | Treasury, GSA, OMB | 2028 |
| X-5 | Payments to excluded parties in any program | Claims and payments matched to all exclusion lists | HHS, OPM, VA, GSA | Late 2027 |
| X-6 | Benefits paid to prisoners | BOP and state prison data | DOJ, SSA, DOL, USDA | 2028 |

The first internal-data pilots rerun Phase 1 ideas on transactions:
**HHS-1** on Medicare claims, **DOD-1** on invoices or **SBA-1** on loan
applications. First agreements by 2027-06, first pilot live by 2027-07-30
(P9), first prosecutor-ready case package by 2027-10.

## Risks and decisions

- **Validation can fail.** Each screen must meet the bar in the review plan
  (known cases five times more common in its top 5%) before its leads are
  used; misses are reported, not hidden.
- **Public data lags** one to two years and hides the claim itself, so
  Phase 1 finds risky entities, not proven fraud.
- **Agreements are the long pole.** If no OIG signs by mid-2027, Phase 2
  slips; that is why proposals go out in week 8.
- **Decisions for Steve** are unchanged from the
  [review plan](review_plan.md): start date and team, agency order,
  commercial data (manifests and corporate registries), referral route
  (OIGs and DOJ only, or False Claims Act filings too), and OIGs versus
  program offices as first Phase 2 partners.
