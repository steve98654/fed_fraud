\hypertarget{bottom-line}{%
\section{Bottom line}\label{bottom-line}}

The agency plans say where fraud is likely and which public-data screens
to run first. These ten extensions supply what a leader asks next: how
the screens work across agencies, what they would return, what a first
engagement looks like, what the law allows, and whether the controls
will survive contact with people trying to beat them. Each has a
planning document; seven have tested reference code; the ROI model and
live demo also have pages on the site.

\hypertarget{how-they-fit-together}{%
\section{How they fit together}\label{how-they-fit-together}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Engine:} cross-agency matching (1) builds the linked
  identities; network analytics (2) groups them into rings; procurement
  (6) and grants to states (7) are new screen families on top of the
  same spine.
\item
  \textbf{Case for action:} the ROI model (3) gives each program a
  business case and a break-even bar; the live demo (8) shows the
  methods running on real data instead of wireframes.
\item
  \textbf{Engagement:} the pilot kit (4) is the 90-day offer; the policy
  and legal track (5) is the map for data access; tip triage (9) is a
  second, small offer that any OIG can accept quickly.
\item
  \textbf{Durability:} the red-team playbook (10) answers ``won't they
  just adapt?'' before anyone asks.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{what-changed-in-the-plan}{%
\section{What changed in the plan}\label{what-changed-in-the-plan}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Shared screens get priority over single-agency builds for
  small programs.} The ROI model shows OPM retirement, FEMA Public
  Assistance and closed pandemic programs do not pay back on their own;
  they should ride on cross-agency matching and exclusion checks.
\item
  \textbf{The first Phase 2 proposal is the HHS 90-day pilot}, with SBA
  as the alternate, using the pilot kit's stop rule and metrics.
\item
  \textbf{Every proposal carries the ROI row, the break-even rate and
  the legal route} from the policy track.
\item
  \textbf{Tip triage is offered as a quick, low-data second engagement}
  with the same OIGs.
\end{enumerate}
